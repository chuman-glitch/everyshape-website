Our approach

Play is how we teach.

ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) is one of the most researched ways to help children build communication, social, and everyday skills. Ours is naturalistic and play-based: learning happens inside real play and daily routines, so new skills carry over to real life.

Community days

Once a month, we take learning out into the world.

Kids get together, try something new, and practice social skills in real places, with our RBTs right there to support them. Community days are for children in our program who are ready for group activities, and for families who'd like to get to know us.
