Our approach

Play is how we teach.

ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) is one of the most researched ways to help children build communication, social, and everyday skills.

Ours is naturalistic and play-based, informed by NET, PRT, and ESDM. Learning happens inside real play and daily routines, so new skills carry over to real life.

  • DrillsDrum solos
  • Flash cardsFinger paint
