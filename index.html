Our approach

Start with what makes each child, themselves.

  1. 01

    Notice their strengths

    We look closely at what a child can already do, what they enjoy, and how they connect with the world.

  2. 02

    Follow their interests

    The things a child already loves are often the most natural place to start building skills and connection.

  3. 03

    Make room for growth

    We focus on goals that matter in everyday life: at the table, at bedtime, at the park, with family.

A simple introduction

What is ABA?

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is an approach to understanding how people learn, and to supporting skills that make daily life easier. In home-based care, learning happens inside a child's own routines and surroundings.

Creativity belongs here

Some children show us what they love by making.

Drawing, building, color, and play can be another way to communicate and connect. We make room for those interests inside each child's support.

Every Shape community days

Once a month, we take learning out into the world.

Kids get together, try something new, and practice social skills in real places, with our RBTs right there to support them.

  • Artist-led workshops

    Local artists host hands-on sessions in painting, making, and building.

  • Field trips

    Dance studios, museums, and other places around town, explored together.

  • Open house for new families

    Curious about our program? Come meet the team and see how we work.

Community days are for children in our program who are ready for group activities, and for families who'd like to get to know us.
