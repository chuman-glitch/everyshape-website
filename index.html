Our approach

Play is how we teach.

ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) is one of the most researched ways to help children build communication, social, and everyday skills. Ours is naturalistic and play-based: learning happens inside real play and daily routines, so new skills carry over to real life.

Pick a way in

Music

The song stops. Your child asks for more. That's a goal, met.

Builds requesting · turn-taking · waiting

Visual arts

Need the blue crayon? Ask for the blue crayon.

Builds asking for help · following directions · fine motor

Dance

Copy the move. Then teach it to us.

Builds imitation · body awareness · joint attention

Theater

The puppet is sad. What should we do?

Builds emotions · conversation · pretend play

Every Shape community days

Once a month, we take learning out into the world.

Kids get together, try something new, and practice social skills in real places, with our RBTs right there to support them.

  • Artist-led workshops

    Local artists host hands-on sessions in painting, making, and building.

  • Field trips

    Dance studios, museums, and other places around town, explored together.

  • Open house for new families

    Curious about our program? Come meet the team and see how we work.

Community days are for children in our program who are ready for group activities, and for families who'd like to get to know us.
