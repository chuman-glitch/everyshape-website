01 The idea

Same goals.
Same data.
Way more glitter.

Montessori changed how children learn at school. We want to do the same for ABA. Children learn best through what they love, with the people they trust most, so we keep the science and change everything around it.

Traditional ABA At Every Shape
Who teaches day to day Mostly a technician You, coached by a BCBA
Where A clinic table Your home and daily routines
How Drills and flash cards Music, art, dance, and play
Hours each week Typical hours Every Shape hours

02 How it works

You lead. Your BCBA coaches. Your child plays.

Your child spends most of their day with you, not with a therapist. So we put our expertise where it lasts: in your hands, inside the routines you already have.

  1. Your BCBA

    Plans and coaches

    What the BCBA does: assessment, the plan, live coaching, reviewing data.

  2. You

    Leads the practice

    What the parent does between sessions, and how long it takes.

  3. Your child

    Picks the game

    Dinosaurs, baking, the color purple. Their interests set the stage, and the goals are woven in.
