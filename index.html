02 How it works

The science of ABA, taught through the things kids love.

Every session is one-on-one with your BCBA, by video, at home. Goals are built into the play, and you're coached each week so progress carries into everyday life.

  1. Your BCBA

    Leads every session

    Works one-on-one with your child by video, builds the plan, and tracks progress.

  2. You

    Joins weekly coaching

    One hour a week with your BCBA, so new skills show up at dinner, at the park, and at bedtime.

  3. Your child

    Picks the game

    Dinosaurs, baking, the color purple. Their interests set the stage, and the goals are woven in.
